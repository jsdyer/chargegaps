SOURCES & METHOD

Coverage is a clue.

A snapshot, not a reservation

Locations come from the official Open Charge Map downloadable country files. Their export date, retrieval date and individual verification dates mean different things. “Operational” is the status recorded in that export; it says nothing about availability now.

Power is a reported rating, sometimes an estimate, not measured charging performance. The power and connector filters must match the same connection entry. Unknown values stay unknown. Connection quantities do not necessarily count independent charging stalls.

Distance has limits

Distances are great-circle straight lines from your reference point, not driving routes. Rivers, mountains, ferries, private access and incompatible connectors can change what is reachable. Filters include private and planned locations unless you select otherwise.

All searches and coordinate calculations stay in this tab. Street-map tiles contact OpenStreetMap and reveal the viewed map area. Turn off Street map to stop new tile requests. Imports are not uploaded or automatically retained; download a capture to keep it.

Make the coverage your own

Search any record or enter any valid coordinates. Add countries, place shortcuts and limits in web/config.json, then run the refresh workflow. Country coverage is recorded in each capture; comparisons skip captures with different coverage.

Data-provider attribution

Open Charge Map aggregates several providers. Only records explicitly marked as open-data licensed, with a stated licence and valid coordinates, are included here. Their individual terms are retained below and in every capture.
